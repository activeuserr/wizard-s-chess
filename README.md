# SHATTERCROWN: Sanctum of Stone
### Gothic Wizard’s Chess — A Material Design 3 Web Experience

**SHATTERCROWN** is a fully 3D, immersive strategic chess game set within a minimalist Gothic cathedral. Built with Three.js, Chess.js, Tone.js, and the Gemini AI API, it bridges classic grandmaster strategy with dark fantasy atmosphere, physics-driven destruction, and real-time AI scrying.

## 🌟 Key Features

1. **Minimalist Gothic Cathedral Environment**: A solemn sanctuary featuring stepped octagonal altar daises, ribbed colonnade pillars, pointed lancet stained-glass arches, and a grand rose window in the apse, complemented by warm candlelight spot-shading and drifting gold dust motes.
2. **Polished Marble vs. Dark Obsidian Pieces**: All 32 chess pieces feature procedural organic stone vein textures, gold/azure trims, and levitating crowns/halos that idly breathe, bob, and rotate. Players can cycle through 4 altar material palettes (**Sanctuary**, **Blood Moon**, **Astral Runes**, and **Reliquary**) and 3 sculpted 3D knight styles (**Destrier**, **Gargoyle**, and **Unicorn**).
3. **Visceral Physics & Voronoi Shatter Explosions**: Non-capturing moves glide smoothly along an elevated levitation arc. Captures trigger a dramatic martial leap, a high-impact strike, and a 34-shard voronoi-style stone shatter explosion with full gravity, floor-bounce physics, and expanding golden/crimson shockwave rings.
4. **Gemini AI Archmage & Tactical Oracle**: Consult the Grand Archmage (`gemini-3-flash-preview`) for structured JSON board divination, live FEN tactical coaching, threat radar warnings, interactive grandmaster chat, and spoken Text-to-Speech (`gemini-2.5-flash-preview-tts`) prophecies using the `Charon` voice model.
5. **Multiplayer & Customization HUD**: Host private 6-character Sanctuary Codes or scan the public queue for random online challengers via a sleek, frosted-glass Material Design 3 single-level dock.
